MenujuCukupAPP

MenujuCukupAPP is a personal desktop tool. It uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. The owner then reviews, edits and publishes each draft inside the TikTok app.

  • Sign in with TikTok (Login Kit)
  • Upload a video from the owner's computer as a draft (Content Posting API)
  • Nothing is published automatically
