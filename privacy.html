Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What the tool accesses

  • Basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), scope user.info.basic
  • Permission to upload videos to your account as drafts, scope video.upload
  • The video files you choose to upload

Where data is stored

Access tokens, refresh tokens, your open ID and an upload log are stored only on your own computer. The developer runs no server that receives this data.

How data is used and shared

Data is used only to sign in and upload your drafts. Video files are sent directly to TikTok. Data is not sold or shared with other third parties. The app can send upload status messages to the owner's own Telegram bot if the owner sets this up; those messages do not contain tokens.

Deleting your data

Remove the app's access in TikTok settings, then delete the token file or the tool folder on your computer.

This website

These pages set no cookies of their own. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits under GitHub's privacy statement.
